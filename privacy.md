# Privacy Policy — Bikers MC Bot

**Last Updated:** 29 September 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
The Bikers MC Bot collects and stores the following information when you interact with it:

- **Discord User ID** — to identify you
- **Discord Username** — for display purposes
- **Application answers** — submitted through the apply form
- **Uploaded images** — for verification purposes (stored temporarily)
- **Server ID** — to identify the server using the Bot

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The collected data is used ONLY to:
- Process your application
- Provide ticket support
- Manage blacklists
- Send notifications related to the Bot's features

## 3. Data Storage
All data is stored locally in an SQLite database on the server hosting the Bot. No data is shared with third parties.

## 4. Data Retention
- **Application data** — deleted after 3 days from acceptance or rejection
- **Blacklist data** — deleted when expired or manually removed
- **Ticket data** — deleted when the ticket is closed

## 5. Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Request a copy of your stored data
- Request deletion of your data

To exercise these rights, contact the Bot owner.

## 6. Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The Bot is not intended for users under 13 years old (or the minimum age in your country).

## 8. Changes to Privacy Policy
This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
